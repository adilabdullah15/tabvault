# TabVault Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026
**Developer:** Adil Abdullah Khan ([GitHub](https://github.com/adilabdullah15))

## What TabVault stores
- Your tabs, windows, workspaces, snapshots and settings are stored **only** in
  `chrome.storage.local` — on your own device.
- This data is **never transmitted** anywhere: no servers, no analytics,
  no tracking, no ads, no accounts.

## Feedback (optional)
- If you use the in-app **Send feedback** button, your name (optional) and your
  message — plus the app version — are sent to the developer's **private**
  Google Sheet. Only the developer can see it.
- If you choose the **email option** instead, your own mail client opens a
  draft addressed to `adilabdullahkhan35@gmail.com`. The extension itself
  sends nothing.

## Permissions used
- `tabs` — to read tab titles/URLs for snapshots and search.
- `storage` — to save your data locally.
- `alarms` — to take automatic snapshots in the background.
- `contextMenus` — for the right-click "save snapshot" menu.
- Host access to `script.google.com` — only to deliver your feedback to the
  private Sheet described above.
